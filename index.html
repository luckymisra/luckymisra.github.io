About

Two national entrance exams, two top‑150 finishes, one seat at India's top research institute.

Pranshu is a computer science graduate student at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, working toward an M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering. He built his engineering foundation at the Bundelkhand Institute of Engineering and Technology, Jhansi, then sat the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering twice — placing among the top 150 computer science candidates nationally on both attempts.

He studies as a Reliance Foundation Scholar and works across machine learning, deep learning, and statistical analysis — with hands-on work in representation learning, NLP, and model fine-tuning, alongside a base in algorithms, systems, and structured problem solving.
